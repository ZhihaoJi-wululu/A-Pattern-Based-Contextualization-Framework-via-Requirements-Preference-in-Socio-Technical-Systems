Diagram keyShapes and relationships
Elements
Actor

An entity with goals and intentions.

Role

A responsibility or function an actor may play.

Agent

A concrete actor with an identity.

Goal

A state or outcome with clear achievement criteria.

Task

An activity or a way to achieve a goal. Its upper-left badge shows its task reference.

Resource

Information or a physical entity needed or provided.

Quality / softgoal

An attribute such as organized materials or privacy. Quality is the iStar 2.0 term; older inputs may call it a softgoal. Its target can be precise or approximate.

Actor / role scope

A dashed boundary groups the actor's own elements. The named symbol sits just outside the top of its scope.

Relationships and annotations
AND refinement

All required children contribute to the parent. AND does not set their order.

OR refinement

Alternative ways to satisfy the parent.

Contribution to a Quality

Make (++) and Help (+) provide sufficient or weak positive evidence. Hurt (−) and Break (−−) provide weak or sufficient negative evidence. These links describe effects on a Quality.

Dependency

A depender relies on a dependee for the goal, task, resource or quality represented by the dependum.

Persona assignment

The concrete persona plays the selected role in the model.

Choice preference

The source alternative is preferred to the target alternative. This is the preference relation added by contextualization.

Input start condition

pre: the source must be attained before the target starts. npr: it must not yet be attained at that point.

Qualification

A Quality describes a desired attribute of a goal, task or resource. The dotted link identifies what the Quality refers to.

NeededBy

A task needs the linked resource, such as an approved file or a piece of equipment.

Names appear inside their symbols. A numbered circle at a task's upper left shows its stable reference, t1…tn. A separate small open circle at the upper right marks an optional element. Task references identify elements; temporal relations express order. play binds the persona to the selected role.
